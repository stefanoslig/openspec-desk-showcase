# Recurring bookings

## Why

Teams hold their stand-ups and one-on-ones in the same room every week. Today a member books each week by hand, and often finds the room taken by the time they get to it. A weekly series books all of them at once.

## What Changes

- A member books a room for the same slot every week, for up to twelve weeks.
- A week in which the room is taken is skipped, and the member is told which one.
- A series can be cancelled whole or one booking at a time.
- A series may run past the 30 days that a single booking can be made ahead.

## Capabilities

| Capability | Change                                                          |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bookings   | Adds weekly series, and lets a series run past the 30-day limit |

## Impact

The booking form, the booking service and the calendar change. Existing bookings stay as they are.
