# Release unattended rooms

## Why

One in five booked rooms stays empty. The room is blocked for the whole slot while other teams look for a place to meet. A booking that nobody checks in to should give the room back.

## What Changes

- A booking that is not checked in ten minutes after its start is released.
- The member who booked is told about the release.

## Capabilities

| Capability | Change                                                        |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Check-in   | Adds the release of unattended rooms and the message about it |

## Impact

The check-in service and the notifications change. The room panels need no update.
