# Show room equipment

## Why

Members book a room and find out at the door that it has no screen or no whiteboard. They then move the meeting or go looking for another room.

## What Changes

- Each room lists its equipment: screen, camera, whiteboard and the number of seats.
- The booking form can filter the rooms by equipment.

This is an early proposal. The requirements follow once the list of equipment is agreed with the office team.
